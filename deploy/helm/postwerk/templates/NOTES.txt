Health, from your machine only:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward deploy/{{ include "postwerk.fullname" . }} 8080:8080
  curl http://127.0.0.1:8080/readyz

Events (one JSON line each) are in the pod log:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs deploy/{{ include "postwerk.fullname" . }} -f
